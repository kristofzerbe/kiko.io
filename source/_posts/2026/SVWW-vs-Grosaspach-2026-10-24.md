It's been exactly seven days since I got to experience a little miracle, and I'm writing about it only today because I simply didn't had time this week ;)

All SVWW fans were hoping that the new coach, Jochen Seitz, would get the team back on track and that the new signings would fit in well within the squad. But what they all conjured up on the pitch last Saturday not only makes us forget the poor start to the season, but also has us dreaming once again of promotion to the 2nd Bundesliga...

<!-- more -->

Yes, it was a really, really good game, and we broke two 3. Liga records in the same game: the most goals in a single game and the most different goal scorers in a single game:

- 16th minute: Marcus **Müller**
- 30th minute: Florian **Hübner**
- 35th minute: Roberto **Massimo**
- 50th minute: Fabian **Greilinger**
- 66th minute: Raul **Paula**
- 72nd minute: Gino **Fechner**
- 91st minute: Robin **Kalem**

What was impressive wasn't just the goals, but the defensive performance combined with a newfound aggressiveness and determination on offense. It was just really exciting to watch, and of course we're not promotion contenders yet, and we'll have our share of bad days and lose games again, but the mere fact that Marcus Müller has finally broken his scoring drought gives me hope that in the club's 101st year, in 2027, we'll be playing against Bochum, Karlsruhe, Hannover, or Pauli in the second division again.

P.S.:
Today's away game against **Verl** just ended, and we won again: **1:4**! We are now unbeaten in six games! I think that's a new club record for the best start by a new coach :D
