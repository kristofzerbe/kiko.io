{% match_grid "SV-Wehen-Wiesbaden" "1:4" "MSV-Duisburg" %}

It's like a curse... on the first matchday after the coach was fired, the guys (finally) scored 2 goals and beat **Fortuna Köln** at home to nil, only then to concede 3 goals at home against **Duisburg** within 15 minutes of the first half just 4 days later. OK, MSV is currently at the top of the standings and on a hot streak, but the ease with which they outplayed our defense was shocking. Like a hot knife through butter.

<!-- more -->

Although the 1-3 goal just before halftime and the team's determination early in the second half were bright spots, but when our new forward [Marcus Müller](https://www.kicker.de/marcus-mueller/spieler/3-liga/2026-27/sv-wehen-wiesbaden) shoots straight at the goalkeeper when he's all alone in front of the goal and the others aren't exactly impressing with their accuracy either, you can't complain about losing to an efficient Duisburg team.

For me, the most memorable scene of the game was the 1-4 goal in the 54th minute. Not only did the Duisburg player tunnel our aforementioned Müller, who, frustrated, just stopped moving, then skillfully dribbled past another player, and had his shot from a tight angle blocked only by a defender's falling body, but the ball rolled right back to him, and he scored from an even tighter angle. Slapstick.

You could see just how frustrated our players were (with themselves) five minutes later. A Duisburg player made a very hard tackle to win the ball and took off sprinting. [Max Brandt](https://www.kicker.de/max-brandt/spieler/3-liga/2026-27/sv-wehen-wiesbaden) had no choice but to tackle him just as aggressively, using his whole body. [Tarik Gözüsirin](https://www.kicker.de/tarik-goezuesirin/spieler/3-liga/2026-27/sv-wehen-wiesbaden), our captain, who had run up with him, smashed the ball into the opponent, who was now lying on the ground. A clear red card and a three-game suspension. Thanks a lot.

Today, they're announcing who the new coach will be. The poor guy has quite a bit of work ahead of him...

---

**Update**: It is ... [**Jochen Seitz**](https://www.kicker.de/jochen-seitz/trainer), the former coach of [Lok Leipzig](https://www.kicker.de/1-fc-lok-leipzig/info), who tragically failed twice in a row to achieve promotion to the third division. Well then, welcome to the 3. Bundesliga ... and please make sure it stays that way, at least.
