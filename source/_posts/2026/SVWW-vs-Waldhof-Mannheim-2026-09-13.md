{% match_grid "SV-Wehen-Wiesbaden" "0:1" "Waldhof-Mannheim" %}

I'm a little ticked off. We had a terrible start to the new season over the first four matchdays; we're in last place with only two points, and on Sunday we had the chance not only to play a good game at home against Mannheim but also to win, since Waldhof was only in 15th place with just 4 points at that time.

<!-- more -->

The coach showed courage in his lineup, starting 4 of the 5 new players ([Massimo](https://www.kicker.de/roberto-massimo/spieler/3-liga/2026-27/sv-wehen-wiesbaden), [Brandt](https://www.kicker.de/max-brandt/spieler/3-liga/2026-27/sv-wehen-wiesbaden), [Müller](https://www.kicker.de/marcus-mueller/spieler/3-liga/2026-27/sv-wehen-wiesbaden) and [Paula](https://www.kicker.de/ raul-paula/players/3rd-division/2026-27/sv-wehen-wiesbaden)) and the guys pretty much had the game under control from the start. The effort and the will to win were definitely there. [Fabian Greilinger](https://www.kicker.de/fabian-greilinger/spieler/3-liga/2026-27/sv-wehen-wiesbaden) made a lasting impression on me with his sprints up and down the field during the entire game, until he was substituted. I think he must have gained some oxygen on the bench.

Then, in the 20th minute, the opposing team produced a little piece of art: After a corner kick, a Mannheim player attempted a bicycle kick but didn't hit the ball cleanly; another player tried to pick up the rebound in the exact same way and scored a spectacular goal to make it **0:1**.

Yo, stuff happens, especially as it was a beautifully scored goal. But what can't happen is that the team continues to dominate the game, yet either fumbles the ball in the penalty area or simply fails to find the goal, which is over 7 meters wide. The constant '*Uuuu*' and '*Oooo*' every time another shot went wide was testing our nerves.

After 85 minutes, my wife and I left, partly because we had other plans, and partly because it was clear to us that the guys definitely wouldn't manage to score today.

The weekend resulted in yet another loss, and a day later, the coach [Daniel Scherning](https://www.kicker.de/daniel-scherning/trainer/3-liga/2026-27/sv-wehen-wiesbaden) was fired. Maybe the team really does need a shot of fresh energy, because things couldn't stay that way. However, I also see cause in the management, as integrating five new players effectively into the team just before or even after the start of the season is difficult, if not impossible. My wife thinks the decision is crap, though... we'll see.
