This new-found enjoyment of the SVWW's play, sparked by the new manager, continued at the weekend. Admittedly, TSV Havelse were by no means a formidable opponent, and given their position in the table (last) we were confident of a victory, but a winning streak will eventually come to an end. 

<!-- more -->

On the bus to the stadium, a little boy was sitting in front of me wearing our colours under his winter jacket, and I asked him, 'Well? 3:0 or 4:0?' To which he replied with great enthusiasm, '10:0!'.

At the start of the match, I was a bit sceptical, because Havelse were playing well and we barely managed to get out of our own half, but they never really created any dangerous chances. Our lads were solid at the back again and cleared everything away. 

After 10 minutes, the play shifted in the other direction and SVWW put together nice combination play up front. It was only Justin Janitzek's shot from 20 metres that seemed to me to be too weak and aimed straight at the goalkeeper – so much so that I'd already turned my head away in annoyance when everyone started cheering! The ball had slipped through the goalkeeper's fingers to make it 1:0! What a whopper...

With the resulting confidence, they played cautiously but consistently, mostly pushing forward against opponents who were somewhat rattled. Roberto Massimo, in particular, repeatedly caused trouble for the Havels' defence, so it came to no surprise that it was he who made it 2:0 shortly after half-time.

The rest of the match was only partly worth watching, as quite a few of the opposition players seemed to have given up and some of our lads appeared to be satisfied with the lead. Not so the substitutes, though. Tarik Gözüsirin came on in the 71st minute and scored the third goal in the 81st minute, assisted by Massimo.

We're now fourth in the table and unbeaten in six matches. Let's hope it continues like this, as we still have eight points to make up on the top spot...
