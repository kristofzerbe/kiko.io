Before yesterday's home match, I was already a bit worried, as Essen had knocked out second-division team St. Pauli in the DFB-Pokal at the weekend, whereas we, as everyone knows, lost 0:4 to Leverkusen. Either a win like that gives a team a boost, or they'll be too tired for their next league match.

<!-- more -->

The last one seemed to be the case, and you could tell our boys were really wanting to win today. That was also down to [Marcus Müller](https://www.kicker.de/marcus-mueller/spieler/3-liga/2026-27/sv-wehen-wiesbaden), our new signing this week, whom the manager, together with Flotho, fielded straight away as a striking duo from the start. A 4-4-2 … that's rare for us too. And how that guy was running! He was clearly intent on making a good impression on the fans straight away, and he accomplished that. I almost felt sorry for one or two of the Essen players when he burst past them.

The rest of the team certainly tried just as hard, but there were simply too many mistakes to benefit from their undoubted determination. Essen were clearly the better team, but still rarely got near goal, and when they did, their shots were harmless. They were tired.

But it wasn't just us who made mistakes, the referee did too, mostly against us, and in such a way that shouts of "cheater" echoed from the stands. The man was either biased or simply not good enough for the 3rd Division.

My personal highlight was the introduction of our second new signing this week in the 58th minute, [Roberto Massimo](https://www.kicker.de/roberto-massimo/spieler/3-liga/2026-27/sv-wehen-wiesbaden), who, less than 30 seconds after stepping onto the pitch, sprinted down the right flank and curled the ball beautifully into the far corner. Unfortunately, the Essen goalkeeper was there to tip the ball over the bar.

Although the final 15 minutes belonged entirely to us, we failed to capitalise on it, which was only partly down to the opponent's good goalkeeper. Yes, there were no goals in this match and we've now got our first point, but it's not exactly satisfying. Things can and must get better, because I certainly won't be buying a season ticket for the Regionalliga.
