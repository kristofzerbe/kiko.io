"Marienburg" located 60 kilometers south of Gdańsk, served for 500 years as the epicenter of the Teutonic Order (Ordo Teutonicus), the German counterpart to the French Knights Templar, who played a major role in the Crusades beginning in 1199 to "liberate" the Holy Land. When there was nothing left to gain there, the knights founded the Teutonic Order State at the end of the 13th century and began to "Christianize" the region, which meant slaying anyone who refused to convert. Later, the state of Prussia emerged from this Order State, naming itself—of all things—after the "Prussians," the very people who had been subjugated first. History's irony.

<!-- more -->

Malbork is by far the largest castle complex in the world and, thanks to its size and history, is an absolute tourist magnet. Of course, we had to check it out, too. It was absolutely fascinating to roam through the restored vaults for hours and imagine what medieval life might have been like within these walls, with the Middle Castle, Upper Castle, and Grand Master's Palace. Even if, like me, you're an atheist, not particularly fond of military organizations, and find the combination of the two simply creepy from an enlightened perspective. But that's just how history is.
